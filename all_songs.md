## 1. Gayatri Mantra / Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra

🎵 **Type** — Devotional / Mantra
🎼 **Raaga** — Gayatri Mantra: Based on Solfeggio frequencies; Maha Mrityunjaya: often sung in Bhairav
🎹 **Composer** — Ancient Vedic scripture (composer: divine origin); Modern renditions by various artists
🎤 **Singer(s)** — Various — popular versions by Pt. Jasraj, Anuradha Paudwal, Lata Mangeshkar, Shankar Mahadevan
🎬 **Film / Album** — Devotional / standalone — featured in many spiritual albums
📅 **Year** — Ancient; modern recordings from 1950s onwards
📝 **Notes** — Gayatri Mantra is addressed to Savitar (Sun deity). Maha Mrityunjaya is a healing mantra of Lord Shiva. Both are among the most sacred Vedic mantras.

**Gayatri Mantra**

Om Bhur Bhuvaḥ Suvaha
Tat-savitur Vareñyam
Bhargo Devasya Dheemahi
Dhiyo Yo Naḥ Prachodayāt

**Maha Mrityunjaya Mantra**

Om Tryambakam Yajamahe
Sugandhim Pushtivardhanam
Urvarukamiva Bandhanan
Mrityor Mukshiya Maamritat
